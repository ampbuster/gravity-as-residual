- H_0,SH0ES = 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc (local, Cepheids + SNe Ia)

The cascade claims the 5.6 km/s/Mpc gap decomposes as:

- Net: 2.88 + 2.84 - 2.76 = 2.96 (NOT 5.6!)

Wait, this doesn't add up. Let me re-check.

The 5.6 km/s/Mpc gap is between Zone 1 (73.04) and Zone 4 (67.4):
- 73.04 - 67.4 = 5.64 ✓

This is the SIMPLE difference, not the sum of perturbations.
The 4-zone picture is:
- H_0,4D = 70.16 (the baseline)
- Zone 1: H = 70.16 + 2.88 = 73.04
- Zone 3: H = 70.16 + 2.84 = 73.00
- Zone 4: H = 70.16 - 2.76 = 67.40

So the 4-zone picture says:
- The "intrinsic" H_0 is 70.16 (geometric mean of 73.04 and 67.4)

- The CMB measurement (67.4) is dragged by -2.76 from the baseline
- Net gap: 2.88 - (-2.76) = 5.64 ✓

So the 4-zone picture CONSISTENTLY accounts for the 5.6 km/s/Mpc gap
as Zone 1 boost + Zone 4 drag relative to the H_0,4D = 70.16 baseline.

This is a CONSISTENT re-description. But it's not a derivation of
WHY the values are 2.88 and -2.76.
