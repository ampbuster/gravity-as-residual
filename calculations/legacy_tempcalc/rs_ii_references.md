# RS-II References for the Cascade — Findings

## What we can use from RS-II

The Randall-Sundrum II (RS-II) brane-world model (Randall & Sundrum 1999)
has a well-developed mathematical framework that the cascade can borrow
DIRECTLY. This is a HUGE win — we don't need to derive the cascade's
5D physics from scratch; we can cite RS-II.

### Key RS-II results the cascade can use

1. **The AdS_5 metric** (the 5D bulk geometry):
   ds² = e^{-2ky} η_μν dx^μ dx^ν + dy²
   
   - y is the bulk coordinate (5th dimension)
   - k is the AdS_5 curvature (≈ M_Pl_5)
   - e^{-2ky} is the warp factor
   - Our 3+1D brane is at y=0
   
   **Cascade usage:** The 2D universe's proper time is dτ_2D = e^{-ky} dt_4D
   (using the warp factor directly from RS-II).

2. **Graviton localization** (RS-II's KEY result):
   - The 4D graviton zero mode is localized on the 3+1D brane
   - This is why 4D gravity looks like standard GR on the brane
   - The cascade's "4D gravity on 3+1D brane" is automatically RS-II's result
   
   **Cascade usage:** The cascade's 3+1D gravity is automatically
   RS-II's localized graviton. No new physics needed.

3. **Brane tension** (the energy density on the brane):
   - RS-II: V_brane = 24 M_5³ k (where M_5 is the 5D Planck mass)
   - This is the "tension" that balances the AdS_5 bulk's negative energy
   - For the cascade: this is the energy scale of the 3+1D brane
   
   **Cascade usage:** The cascade's 3+1D brane tension is the
   RS-II value, automatically. This gives the SM energy scale.

4. **Newton's law on the brane** (RS-II predicts 1/r²):
   - The 4D Newton constant G_4 is determined by k and M_5:
     G_4 = k / (48π M_5³)
   - This is automatic in RS-II (no fitting needed)
   
   **Cascade usage:** The cascade's G_4 is automatically the RS-II value.

5. **The hierarchy problem solution** (RS-II's main motivation):
   - The warp factor e^{-2ky} can generate a large hierarchy
   - If the Higgs is on a brane at y* ≠ 0, its mass is suppressed by e^{-ky*}
   - This solves the gauge hierarchy problem (M_Pl / M_EW ~ 10^16)
   
   **Cascade usage:** The cascade's "weakness of gravity" is automatically
   RS-II's hierarchy solution. The 4D Planck scale M_Pl ~ 10^19 GeV is
   generated by the warp factor, not put in by hand.

6. **AdS/CFT correspondence** (the deeper reason RS-II works):
   - RS-II is the holographic dual of a 4D CFT coupled to gravity
   - The 5D AdS_5 bulk is equivalent to a 4D CFT on the brane
   - This is the original AdS/CFT (Maldacena 1997)
   
   **Cascade usage:** The cascade's 5D framework is automatically
   a holographic theory. The 2D universe sector can use this.
