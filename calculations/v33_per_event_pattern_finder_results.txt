================================================================================
SUMMARY
================================================================================

The brute force formula mu = K * alpha * (E/M_Pl,3D) / tau gives:
  - SN match by construction (K calibrated to SN)
  - Per-event mu varies by 6.32e+14
  - Slope of log mu vs log E is 1, slope vs log tau is -1
  - This is the entropy-matching pattern

CONCLUSION: mu is EVENT-DEPENDENT if we use the entropy-matching formula.
The framework's choice of universal mu = 9e6 GeV^2 is the SN-calibrated value.
No formula involving alpha makes mu universal.
