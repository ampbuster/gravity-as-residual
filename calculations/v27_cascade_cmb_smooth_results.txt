================================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. CONSTANT F_p = 0.7 (§4.48 current) under-predicts Omega_DM(1100) by ~30%
2. STEP FUNCTION (F_p jumps to 1.0 at z=4) gives a clean match at both anchors
3. SMOOTH CURVES (sigmoid, exp, hill) can match both anchors if z_scale is right
4. The 'gap' at z=1100 is bridged by ANY function that grows F_p from 0.7 to ~1.0

PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION:
  - F_p = 0.7 means 70% of DM came from the 4D event's steady activity
  - F_p → 1.0 at high z means early DM was PRIMARILY primordial (no stellar)
  - This is consistent with: 4D event's activity was higher in the past,
    OR early 2D universes were more massive / longer-lived (different E)

Smooth F(z) is more honest than step because:
  - The 4D event's R_p(z) is unlikely to be a step function
  - Stellar activity drops smoothly with z (Madau-SFR)
  - The transition between F_p-dominated and F_s-dominated is gradual

RECOMMENDATION: replace F_p = const with F_p(z) = 0.7 + 0.3 × (1 - exp(-z/z_scale))
  with z_scale ~ 2-4 (transition over z = 0 to z = 8).

This is a 1-parameter family (z_scale) that fits both anchors and provides
a smooth, physically motivated transition.
