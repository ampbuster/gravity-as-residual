CONSEQUENCE 3a: m_2D_i SCALES DIFFERENTLY
================================================================================

For event-dependent μ:
  m_2D_i ∝ μ_i × τ_i² = (K × α × E_i/τ_i) × τ_i²
           = K × α × E_i × τ_i
           = K × α × E_i × (E_i/M_Pl,3D)^α × t_Pl
           = K × α × t_Pl/M_Pl,3D^α × E_i^(1+α)
           = K × α × t_Pl/M_Pl,3D^α × E_i^2.289

For universal μ (v3.3):
  m_2D_i ∝ μ × τ_i² = μ × (E_i/M_Pl,3D)^α × t_Pl × τ_i
            = μ × E_i^α × E_i^α × (t_Pl/M_Pl,3D^α)
            = μ × (t_Pl/M_Pl,3D^α) × E_i^(2α)
            = μ × (t_Pl/M_Pl,3D^α) × E_i^2.578

Both scale strongly with E_i, but:
  Event-dep: E^2.289 (slightly lower power)
  Universal: E^2.578 (slightly higher power)

AGN/quasar still dominate in BOTH cases, but the relative weights differ.

CONSEQUENCE 4: GW BACKGROUND PREDICTION
================================================================================

Each 2D universe birth produces a GW burst (gravitational wave signal)
In v3.3 framework (universal μ):
  - All 2D universes have M_Pl,2D = 3 TeV
  - Same GW frequency spectrum (mostly)
  - Total GW background = Σ_i N_i × GW_i

In v3.3.6 framework (event-dependent μ):
  - Each 2D universe has its own M_Pl,2D
  - Different M_Pl,2D → different GW frequency spectrum
  - TNT 2D universes have M_Pl,2D ~ 360,000 TeV (high frequency)
  - Quasar 2D universes have M_Pl,2D ~ 14 GeV (low frequency)

PREDICTION CHANGE:
  - v3.3: GW background dominated by SN-class events at f ~ M_Pl,2D/c² ~ 10⁹ Hz
  - v3.3.6: GW background has WIDER spectrum (10⁶ - 10¹² Hz)
  - Different observability with SKA, LIGO, etc.
