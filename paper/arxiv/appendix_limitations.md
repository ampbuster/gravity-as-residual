## Honest Framing

The framework's value is partly in its transparency. Rather than hiding limitations, SIDC documents them. This is unusual for "theory of everything" preprints, which typically suffer from overconfidence. SIDC's approach is more like a software development log—every change, every failure, every revision is preserved.
