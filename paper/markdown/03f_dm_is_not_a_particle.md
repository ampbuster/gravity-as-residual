## Why traditional DM tests don't fully apply

Standard DM searches (WIMP, axion, etc.) assume:
- DM is a particle with mass m_DM
- DM can self-annihilate (gives gamma rays)
- DM can scatter off nuclei (direct detection)
- DM has specific cross-sections

The framework's DM:
- May not be a single particle
- Doesn't self-annihilate (no annihilation cross-section)
- May not scatter off nuclei (no specific cross-section)
- Just provides gravitational clustering

So tests like:
- Fermi-LAT gamma-ray lines: don't apply (no annihilation)
- XENON/LZ nuclear recoils: may not apply (no scattering)
- HESS Cherenkov: don't apply (no annihilation)

These tests can't directly probe the framework's DM.
